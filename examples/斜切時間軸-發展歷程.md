# DEVELOPMENT HISTORY
## Apex Line · 2010–2026
從 3 艘散雜貨船到全球前十大貨櫃航商，每一步都在同一條航線上。

- 2010-2014|Setting Sail & Foundation|Founded with 3 bulk carriers. Launched first direct Southeast Asia route.
- 2015-2018|Fleet Expansion|Fleet surpassed 20 vessels. Opened trans-Pacific routes and Singapore hub.
- 2019-2021|Digital Transformation|Implemented smart vessel scheduling. Reduced fuel consumption per TEU.
- 2022-2024|Green Shipping|Ordered methanol dual-fuel ships. Committed to 2050 carbon neutrality.
- 2025-2026|Global Network|Covered 100+ countries. Top 10 global capacity. End-to-end digital supply chain.
